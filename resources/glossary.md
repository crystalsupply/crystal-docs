# Glossary

Key terms and concepts used in CrystalSupply documentation.

## A

**Admin Fee**
- Small percentage (0.1-1%) of mining fees allocated to protocol development and operations
- Goes to admin wallet
- Used for ongoing development, audits, and improvements

**Admin Wallet**
- Address that receives admin fees from mining rounds
- Controlled by protocol team
- Used for development and operational expenses

**APR (Annual Percentage Rate)**
- Estimated yearly return on staked TAL
- Dynamic based on mining activity and total staked
- Does not account for compounding (see APY)

**APY (Annual Percentage Yield)**
- Yearly return including compounding effects
- Higher than APR due to auto-compounding in CrystalSupply staking
- Formula: `APY = (1 + APR/n)^n - 1` where n = compounding frequency

**Auto-Compounding**
- Automatic reinvestment of staking rewards
- Increases your staked balance without manual claiming
- Core feature of CrystalSupply staking contract

## B

**Block (Grid Block)**
- One of 25 positions on the CrystalSupply mining grid (0-24)
- Users stake MON on blocks to participate in mining
- One block randomly selected as winner each round

**Block Explorer**
- Web interface to view blockchain transactions and contract state
- Monad's equivalent to Etherscan
- Used to verify transactions and view contract data

**Buyback**
- Protocol uses vault MON to purchase TAL from market
- Creates buy pressure on TAL
- Purchased TAL distributed to stakers or burned

## C

**Checkpoint**
- Process of calculating and recording user's rewards from a completed round
- Can be done manually or automatically via keepers
- Necessary before claiming rewards

**Claim**
- Withdrawing earned rewards (TAL or MON) to your wallet
- Separate from checkpointing
- Two types: claimToken() and claimMon()

**Crystal (TAL)**
- Native token of CrystalSupply protocol
- Max supply: 21,000,000 TAL
- ERC20 token on Monad blockchain
- Used for staking, governance, and value accrual

**Cumulative Position**
- Running total of MON staked on a block across all users
- Used to calculate proportional rewards
- Tracked in contract state

## D

**Deflationary**
- Economic model where token supply decreases over time
- CrystalSupply is deflationary via halving mechanism
- Contrasts with inflationary models

**Distributor (Reward Distributor)**
- Address authorized to distribute rewards to staking contract
- Typically the CrystalSupply mining contract or buyback contract
- Can add TAL to staking pool to increase share value

## E

**Emission**
- Rate at which new TAL tokens are mined/created
- Decreases over time due to halving
- Measured in TAL per round or TAL per day

**Entropy**
- Randomness used to determine winning block
- Generated by Pyth Entropy V2 oracle
- Cryptographically secure and verifiable

**ERC20**
- Standard interface for fungible tokens on EVM blockchains
- TAL token implements ERC20
- Allows compatibility with wallets and DEXs

**EVM (Ethereum Virtual Machine)**
- Execution environment for smart contracts
- Monad is EVM-compatible
- Allows using Ethereum tooling (MetaMask, Solidity, etc.)

## F

**Fair Launch**
- Token distribution with no pre-mine or insider advantage
- CrystalSupply is 100% fair launch
- All TAL must be mined by community

**Fee (Protocol Fee)**
- Percentage taken from mining stakes
- 11% for winners, 1.1% for losers
- Split between vault and admin

**Finalized (Round)**
- Round that has completed and determined winner
- Rewards calculated and available for checkpoint
- Cannot be modified after finalization

## G

**Gas**
- Fee paid for executing transactions on blockchain
- Paid in MON on Monad
- Required for mining, claiming, staking operations

**Grid**
- 5x5 arrangement of 25 blocks used in mining
- Visual representation of mining options
- Each block has equal chance of winning

**Gwei**
- Unit of measurement for gas prices
- 1 MON = 1,000,000,000 Gwei
- Used to set transaction priority

## H

**Halving**
- Event where mining rewards are cut in half
- Occurs every 3,000,000 TAL mined
- Creates scarcity and deflationary pressure

**Halving Era**
- Period between halvings
- Era 0: 32 TAL/round, Era 1: 16 TAL/round, etc.
- Maximum 6 halvings planned

## I

**Initial Circulating**
- 105,000 TAL minted at protocol launch
- Used to bootstrap liquidity
- Represents 0.5% of max supply

**Intermission**
- Brief period (~12 seconds) after round ends
- Time for randomness generation and finalization
- No prospecting allowed during intermission

## K

**Keeper**
- Automated bot that checkpoints user rewards
- Charges small fee (default 0.1 MON)
- Optional service for convenience

**Keeper Fee**
- Fee paid to keepers for auto-checkpointing
- Configurable by protocol (default 0.1 MON)
- Deducted from your MON refunds

## L

**Liquidity Mining**
- Earning rewards by providing liquidity to DEX pools
- Future feature for TAL/MON pairs
- Complements mining and staking

**Loser (Mining)**
- User whose block was not selected as winner
- Gets 98.9% of MON stake back
- No TAL rewards, but minimal loss

## M

**Max Supply**
- Hard cap on total TAL tokens: 21,000,000
- Cannot be increased
- Enforced by smart contract

**Mining**
- Process of earning TAL by staking MON on grid blocks
- Core mechanism of CrystalSupply
- Round-based with VRF randomness

**Minter**
- Address authorized to mint new TAL tokens
- Only the CrystalSupply contract
- Set in Crystal token contract

**MON**
- Native cryptocurrency of Monad blockchain
- Used for gas fees and mining stakes
- Refunded after mining rounds (minus fees)

**Monad**
- High-performance EVM-compatible blockchain
- 10,000+ TPS, sub-second finality
- Home of CrystalSupply protocol

**Motherlode**
- Rare event (~0.1% chance) with massive bonus rewards
- Jackpot-style feature
- Extra incentive for winners

## P

**Pre-mine**
- Tokens created before public launch
- CrystalSupply has NO pre-mine
- All TAL must be mined fairly

**Prospect**
- Action of staking MON on a grid block
- Main mining interaction
- Function: `prospect(roundId, blockIdx, amount)`

**Protocol Vault**
- Smart contract holding protocol fees
- Receives 10% of mining fees
- Used for buybacks and staking rewards

**Pyth Entropy**
- Decentralized oracle providing verifiable randomness
- Powers CrystalSupply's fair mining
- Version 2 (V2) used

## R

**Randomness**
- Unpredictable number used to select winning block
- Generated by Pyth Entropy V2
- Verifiable and transparent

**Refined TAL**
- TAL that has completed refining period
- Claimable without penalty
- Contrasts with unrefined TAL

**Refining Fee**
- 10% fee on unrefined TAL claimed early
- Encourages patience
- Waived after refining period

**Reentrancy**
- Type of smart contract attack
- CrystalSupply protected via ReentrancyGuard
- All state-changing functions are nonReentrant

**Round**
- Single mining cycle (~60 seconds)
- Users prospect, randomness selects winner, rewards distributed
- Tracked by roundId

**Round Duration**
- Length of active prospecting phase
- Default: 60 seconds
- Configurable by governance

## S

**Sequence Number**
- Identifier for Pyth Entropy randomness requests
- Maps to specific round
- Used to track VRF callbacks

**Shares**
- Units representing your portion of staking pool
- Minted when depositing TAL
- Burned when withdrawing TAL
- Value increases as rewards compound

**Share Price**
- Current value of one share in TAL
- Formula: `Total Pooled TAL / Total Shares`
- Increases as rewards are distributed

**Slippage**
- Difference between expected and actual price
- Relevant when buying/selling TAL on DEX
- Set slippage tolerance in wallet

**Stake (Staking)**
- Locking TAL in staking contract to earn rewards
- Auto-compounding mechanism
- No lock-up period

**Stake (Mining)**
- Amount of MON placed on a block during prospect
- Determines your share of rewards if block wins
- Refunded after round (minus fees)

## T

**TAL**
- Ticker symbol for Crystal token
- Max supply: 21,000,000
- Primary asset of CrystalSupply

**Token Pot**
- Accumulated TAL from previous rounds
- Distributed in future rounds
- Increases reward variance

**Tokenomics**
- Economic model of TAL token
- Includes supply, distribution, halving, fees
- Designed for sustainability and fairness

**Total Pooled TAL**
- Sum of all TAL in staking contract
- Increases with reward distributions
- Used to calculate share price

**Total Shares**
- Sum of all shares issued to stakers
- Increases when users deposit
- Decreases when users withdraw

**Treasury**
- Another term for Protocol Vault
- Holds fees and manages buybacks
- Key component of protocol sustainability

## U

**Unclaimed Rewards**
- Rewards earned but not yet claimed
- Tracked per user
- Separate tracking for TAL and MON

**Unrefined TAL**
- TAL that hasn't completed refining period
- Subject to 10% fee if claimed early
- Automatically refines over time

## V

**Vault Fee**
- Portion of mining fees going to protocol vault
- 10% for winners, 1% for losers
- Largest protocol revenue stream

**Verifiable Random Function (VRF)**
- Cryptographic method for generating provable randomness
- Pyth Entropy V2 is a VRF
- Ensures fairness in CrystalSupply mining

## W

**Wallet**
- Software for managing cryptocurrency
- Examples: MetaMask, Rabby, Trust Wallet
- Required to interact with CrystalSupply

**Web3**
- Decentralized internet powered by blockchain
- CrystalSupply is a Web3 application
- Requires Web3 wallet to use

**Winner**
- User whose block was selected in a round
- Receives TAL rewards proportional to stake
- Pays 11% fee on MON stake

**Winner Take All (WTA)**
- Rare round mode (~1% chance)
- Only winners get TAL
- Losers get full MON refunds minus small fee
- Higher variance and excitement

**Winning Block**
- Block (0-24) randomly selected each round
- All users who prospected this block share TAL rewards
- Determined by Pyth Entropy randomness

## Y

**Yield**
- Return earned from staking or liquidity providing
- In CrystalSupply, refers to staking rewards
- Auto-compounding for maximum efficiency
