# Glossary

Quick reference for CrystalSupply terminology and concepts.

## A

**Admin Fee** - Small percentage (0.1-1%) of mining fees allocated to protocol development

**APR (Annual Percentage Rate)** - Estimated yearly return on staked TAL

**Auto-Compounding** - Automatic reinvestment of staking rewards without manual claiming

## B

**Block (Grid Block)** - One of 25 positions on the CrystalSupply mining grid (0-24)

**Buyback** - Protocol purchases TAL from market using vault MON

## C

**Checkpoint** - Process of calculating and recording user rewards from a completed round

**Claim** - Withdrawing earned rewards (TAL or MON) to your wallet

**Crystal (TAL)** - Native token of CrystalSupply, max supply 21,000,000

## D

**Deflationary** - Economic model where token supply growth decreases over time via halving

## E

**Emission** - Rate at which new TAL tokens are mined/created

**Entropy** - Randomness generated by Pyth Entropy V2 oracle for fair mining

**ERC20** - Standard interface for fungible tokens on EVM blockchains

**EVM** - Ethereum Virtual Machine, execution environment for smart contracts

## F

**Fair Launch** - Token distribution with no pre-mine or insider advantage

**Fee (Protocol Fee)** - Percentage taken from mining stakes (11% winners, 1.1% losers)

**Finalized (Round)** - Round that has completed and determined winner

## G

**Gas** - Fee paid for executing transactions on blockchain, paid in MON

**Grid** - 5x5 arrangement of 25 blocks used in mining

## H

**Halving** - Event where mining rewards are cut in half every 3M TAL mined

**Halving Era** - Period between halvings (Era 0: 32 TAL/round, Era 1: 16 TAL/round, etc.)

## I

**Initial Circulating** - 105,000 TAL minted at protocol launch for bootstrapping liquidity

**Intermission** - Brief period (~12 seconds) after round ends for randomness generation

## K

**Keeper** - Automated bot that checkpoints user rewards for a small fee

**Keeper Fee** - Fee paid to keepers for auto-checkpointing (default 0.1 MON)

## M

**Max Supply** - Hard cap on total TAL tokens: 21,000,000

**Mining** - Process of earning TAL by staking MON on grid blocks

**Minter** - Address authorized to mint new TAL (only CrystalSupply contract)

**MON** - Native cryptocurrency of Monad blockchain

**Monad** - High-performance EVM-compatible blockchain (10,000+ TPS)

**Motherlode** - Rare event (~0.1% chance) with massive bonus rewards

## P

**Pre-mine** - Tokens created before public launch (CrystalSupply has NONE)

**Prospect** - Action of staking MON on a grid block to participate in mining

**Protocol Vault** - Smart contract holding protocol fees for buybacks and rewards

**Pyth Entropy** - Decentralized oracle providing verifiable randomness

## R

**Randomness** - Unpredictable number generated by Pyth Entropy V2 to select winning block

**Refined TAL** - TAL that has completed refining period, claimable without penalty

**Refining Fee** - 10% fee on unrefined TAL claimed early

**Round** - Single mining cycle (~60 seconds) where users prospect and winner is determined

**Round Duration** - Length of active prospecting phase (default 60 seconds)

## S

**Shares** - Units representing your portion of staking pool, value increases as rewards compound

**Share Price** - Current value of one share in TAL (Total Pooled TAL / Total Shares)

**Stake (Staking)** - Locking TAL in staking contract to earn auto-compounding rewards

**Stake (Mining)** - Amount of MON placed on a block during prospect

## T

**TAL** - Ticker symbol for Crystal token, max supply 21,000,000

**Token Pot** - Accumulated TAL from previous rounds distributed in future rounds

**Tokenomics** - Economic model of TAL including supply, distribution, halving, and fees

**Total Pooled TAL** - Sum of all TAL in staking contract

**Total Shares** - Sum of all shares issued to stakers

## U

**Unclaimed Rewards** - Rewards earned but not yet claimed by user

**Unrefined TAL** - TAL that hasn't completed refining period (10% fee if claimed early)

## V

**Vault Fee** - Portion of mining fees going to protocol vault (10% winners, 1% losers)

**VRF (Verifiable Random Function)** - Cryptographic method for generating provable randomness

## W

**Wallet** - Software for managing cryptocurrency (MetaMask, Rabby, etc.)

**Web3** - Decentralized internet powered by blockchain technology

**Winner** - User whose block was randomly selected in a round, receives TAL rewards

**Winner Take All (WTA)** - Rare round mode (~1% chance) where only winners get TAL

**Winning Block** - Block (0-24) randomly selected each round via Pyth Entropy

## Y

**Yield** - Return earned from staking or providing liquidity
